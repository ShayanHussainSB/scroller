# Code of Conduct

Scroller follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be kind, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't tolerated in issues, pull requests or anywhere else in the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, contact the maintainer privately, [@ShayanHussainSB](https://github.com/ShayanHussainSB), through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or block participants who break this code.
