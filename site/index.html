Two minutes. Once.

Scroller isn't in the Chrome Web Store or on Firefox Add-ons yet, so you load it yourself from the latest release. Do it once; your settings carry over every update.

Chrome

And Edge, Brave, Arc, Opera, Vivaldi.

  1. Download scroller-chrome.zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode, top right.
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  5. Pin Scroller from the puzzle-piece menu. Open a manga. Press S.
Download for Chrome

Firefox

Version 140 or newer.

  1. Download scroller-firefox.zip. No need to unzip it.
  2. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox.
  3. Click Load Temporary Add-on… and pick the zip.
  4. Pin Scroller from the Extensions menu. Open a manga. Press S.

Firefox forgets add-ons Mozilla hasn't signed when it restarts, so load it again after a restart. Signed builds are planned.

Questions.

Is it free?

Yes. Scroller is free and open source under the MIT license. No accounts, no ads, no paid tier.

Does it work on my phone?

Scroller is a desktop browser extension: Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, Opera, Vivaldi and Firefox 140 or newer. On your phone, send yourself the link and install it on your computer.

Will it work on my manga or novel site?

It runs on any website, including readers that use CSS smooth scrolling and readers that scroll an inner panel instead of the page. Browser pages like chrome:// and the extension stores don't allow extensions at all.

How does Next chapter find the button?

It scores every visible link and button on its words, label, class names and rel="next". Anything that says prev or back is skipped. If it can't find one, can't tell which of two arrows goes forward, or the click doesn't move the page on, it stops rather than going back or looping. The next chapter has to be on the same website.

How do words per minute work?

On text pages Scroller switches to words per minute by itself and measures how much room a word takes in that page's own font, line height and column, so 250 wpm is 250 words a minute on any site, at any zoom. Chinese and Japanese count characters at a matched pace.

Why isn't it in the Chrome Web Store?

Not yet. For now you download it from the latest GitHub release and load it yourself, which takes a couple of minutes, once. Signed Firefox builds are planned.

Does it collect anything?

No. No accounts, tracking, analytics or network requests. Your settings and per-site speeds live in your browser's local extension storage.

Nothing happens when I press S.

Open the popup. If it says Can't reach this page, click Reload tab: pages opened before you installed or updated don't have Scroller yet. In Firefox, allow Scroller to access all websites under about:addons, Scroller, Permissions.
